# SYNTHETIC AUTO POLICY (DEMO ONLY — NOT A REAL INSURER)

**Purpose:** Practice document-grounded Q&A. This text is entirely fictional.

## Article I — Collision Coverage (Section COLL-1)

Collision coverage pays for damage to **your covered vehicle** caused by a collision with another vehicle or object, including rollovers, subject to your **deductible of $500** unless you selected a different amount on the declarations page.

**Exclusions under COLL-1:** We do not pay for:
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or road damage to tires only.
- Damage that occurred while the vehicle was used for **commercial racing**.
- Loss when the vehicle was **intentionally** damaged by the named insured.

## Article II — Rental Reimbursement (Section RR-2)

If you have purchased Rental Reimbursement, we will pay up to **$40 per day** for a rental vehicle, for a **maximum of 30 days**, while your covered vehicle is being repaired due to a covered collision or comprehensive loss.

**RR-2 does not apply** if the loss is not covered under collision or comprehensive, or if the repair time is under 24 hours.

## Article III — Glass Repair (Section GL-3)

**Comprehensive** coverage includes repair or replacement of safety glass without applying the deductible, **once per policy period**, when you use an **approved glass vendor** listed in the mobile app.

## Article IV — Emergency Roadside (Section ER-4)

Roadside assistance covers **towing up to 15 miles** to the nearest qualified repair facility, battery jump, flat tire change (you must have a spare), and lockout service — **limited to 4 service calls per policy period**.
